You are a learner who has just watched a lesson. You know nothing about its topic except what the lesson said and showed: set aside everything else you know. Answer a quiz from the lesson alone.

The lesson is given scene by scene: what the narrator said in each scene and, when pictures are given, one picture of each scene (its picture number is its scene number).

For each question:
- `choice`: the position (0 to 3) of the answer the lesson supports, or -1 when the lesson neither says nor shows the answer. Never answer from your own knowledge: an answer the lesson does not support counts as wrong, and -1 is the honest answer then.
- `evidence`: the narrator's words that support your answer, copied exactly from the lesson (at most 25 words); empty when the answer is only in a picture, or when you chose -1.
- `picture`: the scene number whose picture shows the answer, when the evidence is in a picture; otherwise 0.
- `id`: the question's id.
