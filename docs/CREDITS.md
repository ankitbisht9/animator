# Credits

## Drawing packs (ADR-066)

The drawings of the `bioicons` pack (1011 drawings, built 2026-10-29) come from
[Bioicons](https://bioicons.com) ([source](https://github.com/duerrsimon/bioicons)), a library of open-licensed scientific
illustrations collected by Simon Dürr and contributors. Each drawing was converted to the app's drawing format
(shapes fitted to a box, colours from its style sheet, gradients as their middle colour, text and images left
out): the drawings used in lessons are adapted. A lesson that places a drawing whose licence asks for credit
lists it, with its author, licence and a link to the original, under **Drawing credits** on the lesson page.
